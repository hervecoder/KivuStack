# KivuStack Ltd

A software and technology company building useful, reliable, and accessible digital products.

Building practical software and digital products. Flagship product: **Perime App** — a digital learning product for Rwanda's road rules examination, now available on Google Play.

## About KivuStack

KivuStack Ltd is a software and technology company focused on designing and developing practical digital solutions.

We build software that is useful, reliable, and accessible — with an emphasis on clarity, performance, and long-term maintainability. Our work spans applications and platforms intended to serve real needs for individuals, businesses, and communities.

**CEO: Herve NDATIMANA** — Chief Executive Officer & Software Developer, leading KivuStack Ltd's development of practical software and digital products, including its flagship product Perime App.

## Mission

To build useful technology that solves real-world problems and creates lasting value for the people and organizations we serve.

## Vision

To become a trusted technology company recognised for practical innovation, dependable products, and sustainable impact.

## What We Build

KivuStack focuses on general-purpose software and technology areas, including:

- **Software applications** — purpose-built applications for specific use cases
- **Web platforms** — accessible, performant web experiences
- **Mobile applications** — reliable mobile products for everyday use
- **Digital products** — tools and services that simplify work and communication
- **Automation and technology solutions** — systems that reduce manual effort and improve reliability

We define and scope each initiative based on real requirements. Our flagship product Perime App is now officially launched and available on Google Play.

### Perime App — Officially launched

**Perime App** — *a flagship product of KivuStack Ltd* — is a digital learning product designed to help users prepare for Rwanda's road rules examination. **Now available on Google Play.** Built by KivuStack Ltd.

- **Status:** Officially launched — Available on Google Play
- **Platform:** Android
- **Package ID:** `com.perime.perimeapp`
- **Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.perime.perimeapp&pcampaignid=web_share

## Technology

KivuStack works with modern software technologies and selects tools based on the requirements of each product.

We prioritise maintainability, security, and developer experience, and we document the technology choices for each project within its own repository.

No single fixed stack is assumed for all KivuStack projects.

## Principles

- **User-focused engineering** — build for real needs, not complexity
- **Simplicity** — prefer clear solutions over clever ones
- **Reliability** — ship stable, testable, and maintainable software
- **Security** — handle data and access with care
- **Continuous improvement** — iterate based on feedback and evidence
- **Open collaboration where appropriate** — share, document, and work in the open when it adds value
