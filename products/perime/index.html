About Perime App

Digital learning for Rwanda's road rules.

Perime App is a flagship product of KivuStack Ltd — now officially launched and available on Google Play.

Perime App helps users learn Rwanda’s road rules and prepare for the driving licence examination.

Perime App presents learning material and practice support for candidates studying Rwanda's road rules. The product is built with clarity and reliability as core goals — consistent with how KivuStack Ltd builds all its digital products. Now available as an Android app on Google Play.
