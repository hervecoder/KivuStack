# Mission and Vision

## Mission

To build useful technology that solves real-world problems and creates lasting value for the people and organizations we serve.

We pursue this by developing practical, well-engineered software and by focusing on long-term value over short-term complexity.

## Vision

To become a trusted technology company recognised for practical innovation, dependable products, and sustainable impact.

Our direction is to grow a portfolio of products that are:

- Useful in daily use
- Reliable over time
- Accessible to a broad audience
- Maintainable and secure by design

## Principles in practice

Our principles — user-focused engineering, simplicity, reliability, security, continuous improvement, and open collaboration where appropriate — guide how we define, build, and operate products.

These statements will be revisited as KivuSoft evolves and will be updated in this repository when they change.
