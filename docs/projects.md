**Perime App — Officially launched — Available on Google Play**

- **Product:** Perime App — a flagship product of KivuStack Ltd · Built by KivuStack Ltd
- **Description:** A digital learning product designed to help users prepare for Rwanda's road rules examination.
- **Status:** Officially launched — Android app available on Google Play
- **Product page:** https://kivustack.pages.dev/products/perime/
- **Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.perime.perimeapp&pcampaignid=web_share
- **Package ID:** `com.perime.perimeapp`
- **Platform:** Android

Perime is an independent educational application intended to help users prepare for the driving licence examination.

KivuStack Ltd is developing additional digital products that will be introduced as they reach public launch. Each product will have its own repository and/or documentation describing its purpose, status, and usage where applicable.

When a product is ready for public reference, it is listed here with:

- Status (e.g., in development, launched, available on Google Play)
- Documentation link
- Official store listing where applicable

We do not list unverified, speculative, or unannounced products.
