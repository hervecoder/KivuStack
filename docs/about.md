# About KivuStack Ltd

**KivuStack Ltd** is a software and technology company.

## Who we are

KivuStack Ltd is establishing its identity as a builder of practical digital products. We are at the foundation stage — defining our company hub, documentation, and initial product direction.

## What we do

We design and develop digital solutions across general software areas:

- Software applications
- Web platforms
- Mobile applications
- Digital products
- Automation and technology solutions

Each product is scoped individually based on real-world requirements and user needs.

## How we work

We focus on:

- **Usefulness** — solving real problems
- **Reliability** — building systems that work consistently
- **Accessibility** — making technology usable and approachable

We choose technologies pragmatically and document decisions per project. We aim to communicate clearly, ship iteratively, and improve continuously.

## Current status

KivuStack is building its digital products and technology ecosystem. Official products, services, and company channels will be announced in this repository as they are established.
