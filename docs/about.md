# About KivuStack Ltd

**KivuStack Ltd** is a software and technology company.

## Who we are

KivuStack Ltd builds practical digital products. We are establishing our company hub, documentation, and product pipeline — with our flagship product **Perime App** now Officially launched and available on Google Play.

## What we do

We design and develop digital solutions across general software areas:

- Software applications
- Web platforms
- Mobile applications
- Digital products
- Automation and technology solutions

Each product is scoped individually based on real-world requirements and user needs.

## How we work

We focus on:

- **Usefulness** — solving real problems
- **Reliability** — building systems that work consistently
- **Accessibility** — making technology usable and approachable

We choose technologies pragmatically and document decisions per project. We aim to communicate clearly, ship iteratively, and improve continuously.

## Current status

KivuStack is building its digital products and technology ecosystem.

**Featured Product:** **Perime App** — *a flagship product of KivuStack Ltd* — is a digital learning product designed to help users prepare for Rwanda's road rules examination. **Now available on Google Play** as an Android app.

- Product page: https://kivustack.pages.dev/products/perime/
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.perime.perimeapp&pcampaignid=web_share
- Package ID: `com.perime.perimeapp`

Perime is an independent educational application intended to help users prepare for the driving licence examination.

Additional official products, services, and company channels will be announced in this repository as they are established.
