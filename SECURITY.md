# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The **KivuSoft Ltd** team encourages responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities.

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in this repository, please:

- Do not publicly disclose exploitable details before a fix has been made available
- Do not post sensitive vulnerability details in public GitHub issues
- Report the issue responsibly so it can be reviewed and remediated

KivuSoft Ltd will review submitted reports and work toward remediation as appropriate.

## Contact

> Official security reporting instructions and contact details will be published here once KivuSoft establishes its dedicated security contact channel.

Until a dedicated channel is available, please avoid posting exploitable details publicly. If you are unsure how to report, you may open a minimal GitHub issue that describes that a potential issue exists without including sensitive details, and we will provide guidance once the official channel is published.

## Scope

This policy applies to the contents of the `hervecoder/KivuSoft` company hub repository. Security considerations for future KivuSoft products will be documented in their respective repositories.

## No bounty or guarantees

This repository does not offer a bug bounty program and does not guarantee specific response times. We do not claim that a formal security team currently operates beyond the review of reports submitted through the future official channel.

Thank you for helping keep KivuSoft Ltd and its community safe.
